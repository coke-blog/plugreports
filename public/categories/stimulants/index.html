Stimulants

Cocaine, meth, speed and the cathinone family — energy at a cardiovascular price. Stimulants drive heart rate, blood pressure and body temperature up. Overdose looks like a heart event: chest pain, racing pulse, overheating.

2xstimulant-involved deaths roughly doubled in 5 yrs (US CDC)
