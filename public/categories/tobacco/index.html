Tobacco & Nicotine

Cigarettes, vapes, snus, hookah and the nicotine products hooking the next generation. Nicotine is the most widely used addictive drug on earth — and the only one still marketed legally to adults everywhere. Most smokers start before 18. Every form carries its own harm profile: combustion cancer, nicotine pouches rewiring adolescent brains, hookah's hour-long smoke sessions.

8Mdeaths per year worldwide from tobacco (WHO)
