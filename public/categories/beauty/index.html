Beauty Industry & Med Spa

Botox parties, black-market filler, and the unregulated cosmetic procedures targeting under-25s. The beauty industry has a drug problem: unlicensed injectors, counterfeit Botox, illegal filler sold online, and teenagers getting lip filler at kitchen-table parties. These substances and procedures carry real risk — vascular occlusion, blindness, sepsis, and worse.

73%of FDA adverse-event reports for dermal fillers come from unlicensed/non-medical settings
