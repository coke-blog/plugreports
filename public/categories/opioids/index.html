Opioids

Heroin, fentanyl, painkillers and the nitazenes — the deadliest category in the drug supply. Opioids suppress breathing. Most overdose deaths in the US, Canada and Europe now involve fentanyl or its analogues — often mixed into drugs the buyer never asked for.

~80%of US overdose deaths involve an opioid (CDC)
