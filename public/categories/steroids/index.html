Steroids & Anabolics

Testosterone, trenbolone, Dianabol and the gym-pharma grey market — what the labels actually contain. Anabolic steroids are the most counterfeited drug class on earth. Underdosed vials, mislabeled esters and contaminated injectables are the norm in grey-market supply — every profile covers the real compound, the risks, and how the brands map.

~50%of grey-market steroids tested are underdosed or fake (independent lab analyses)
