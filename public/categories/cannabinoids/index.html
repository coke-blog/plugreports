Cannabinoids

Natural cannabis and synthetic 'Spice' — same receptors, wildly different dangers. Natural cannabis is one of the most-used drugs on earth — low acute toxicity but real risks with potency, dependence and contaminants. Synthetic cannabinoids ('Spice', K2) bind far harder than THC: seizures, catatonia, kidney failure, deaths.

100x+potency of some synthetics vs natural THC
