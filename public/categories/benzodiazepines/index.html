Benzodiazepines

Xanax, Valium and the research-chemical benzos — calm that quietly becomes dependence. Benzodiazepine withdrawal can kill (seizures). Never stop abruptly after regular use — medical tapering is essential.

~30%of opioid overdoses also involve benzos (NIDA)
