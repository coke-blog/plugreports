Meth in Africa: the cities where 'tik' rules

Africa's meth story is a South African story — Cape Town's 'tik' epidemic, with Nigeria, Mozambique and Kenya rising behind it.

21.4%of South African treatment admissions are for meth — the #2 substance nationally (Central Drug Authority 2022)
26African countries reported meth seizures 2011–21 — nearly triple the previous decade (UNODC)
MOZ + SAthe largest aggregate meth seizures in Africa over the past decade

How this ranking works

Methamphetamine arrived in Africa through one city: Cape Town, where locally made 'tik' went from unknown in 2003 to the city's main drug of abuse within five years. South Africa remains the continent's meth heartland — for use, production and as a trafficking springboard. Behind it, UNODC data shows meth seizures reported by 26 African countries, with Mozambique and Nigeria recording the largest aggregate quantities and a growing West African consumer market.

Read this firstNo country anywhere measures meth use per city directly. This ranking combines the strongest available proxies — wastewater analysis, forensic-lab reporting, treatment admissions and police seizures — and is directional, not a precise league table. Positions in the middle of each list should be read as a band.
#CityWhy it ranks here
1Cape Town, SAThe original African meth city. Tik is the most common primary drug among under-20s entering treatment in the Western Cape (over half of admissions in recent data), community studies have found lifetime use above 10% among school learners, and the city's gang-controlled distribution networks remain the template for the whole continent's trade.
2Gqeberha (Port Elizabeth) / Nelson Mandela Bay, SAThe Eastern Cape's tik capital; treatment data shows the province second only to the Western Cape in meth admissions.
3Johannesburg / Pretoria, SAGauteng's size makes it the largest absolute meth market; it is also the hub where tik meets the rest of Africa — Johannesburg records the country's largest population of people who inject drugs.
4Durban, SAKwaZulu-Natal's port city; a documented meth and heroin transshipment point with a significant local user population.
5Lagos, NigeriaWest Africa's emerging meth center: Nigeria records the third-largest aggregate meth seizures in Africa, with Lagos' markets and ports at the center of both import and — increasingly — domestic production.
6Abuja / northern NigeriaNigerian drug-enforcement reporting shows meth production and use spreading north of the capital.
7Maputo, MozambiqueMozambique records Africa's largest aggregate meth seizures alongside South Africa; Maputo's port is the continent's biggest meth entry point for Asian supply.
8Nairobi / Mombasa, KenyaKenya's coastal and capital markets: meth ('banga') is the rising stimulant behind the region's dominant heroin trade, with Mombasa' port as the entry point.
9Windhoek, NamibiaNamibia shares South Africa's tik market; treatment and police data show meth as the country's leading hard drug.
10Accra, GhanaGhanaian reporting shows growing meth seizures and local production (including 'kush'-adjacent stimulant mixtures) in the capital region.

Also worth knowing

Continental caveat: almost no African country has modern wastewater monitoring or household drug surveys. This ranking leans on treatment admissions (South Africa's SACENDU/Central Drug Authority data is the best on the continent), seizure geography from UNODC, and targeted academic studies.

Methodology

Methodology: South African Central Drug Authority treatment statistics (2022–24), SACENDU monitoring, UNODC World Drug Report seizure data by country (2011–2023), and peer-reviewed community studies. Rankings outside South Africa are inferred from seizure and treatment geography — treat as directional.
