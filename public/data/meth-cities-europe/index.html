Top 10 European cities with the most methamphetamine use

Europe's meth map is a Czech story — with Slovak, German and Baltic spillover. Ranked from the SCORE/EUDA wastewater study covering 115–130 cities.

130European cities monitored in the 2025 SCORE wastewater study
Top 5Czech cities occupy the first five meth positions in Europe
+46%of 80 monitored cities saw meth loads rise 2024–25

How this ranking works

Europe actually has the best city-level meth data in the world: the SCORE group's annual wastewater study, published with the European Union Drugs Agency, measures methamphetamine metabolites in the sewage of 115–130 cities across 28 countries. The 2025 results are unambiguous: Europe's meth use is concentrated in Czechia, with Slovakia, eastern Germany, the Baltics, Norway and parts of Spain and Türkiye forming a second tier. Unlike cocaine or MDMA, meth loads are flat through the week — a signature of dependent, daily use rather than party use.

Read this firstNo country anywhere measures meth use per city directly. This ranking combines the strongest available proxies — wastewater analysis, forensic-lab reporting, treatment admissions and police seizures — and is directional, not a precise league table. Positions in the middle of each list should be read as a band.
#CityWhy it ranks here
1České Budějovice, CZTook the #1 spot in the 2025 SCORE ranking after a ~29% year-on-year jump, displacing Ústí nad Labem. Czechia's homegrown meth tradition — 'pervitin' — dates to the pre-1989 era, and small domestic labs still feed the market alongside industrial-scale imports.
2Ústí nad Labem, CZThe 2024 #1 and perennial top-two; the highest historical meth loads ever recorded in the study come from Czech industrial cities near the German border.
3Prague, CZThe capital rounds out the all-Czech top tier; all measured substances increased in Prague in the latest round.
4Plzeň / Ostrava, CZCzech cities occupy positions 4–5 across the study, giving Czechia the strongest per-capita meth market in Europe by an order of magnitude.
5Bratislava, SKSlovakia's capital; high meth loads reflecting the shared Czech-Slovak 'pervitin' market, plus sharply rising amphetamine ('speed') use.
6Chemnitz / Erfurt, DEEastern German cities showing Czech-style meth loads — attributed by Czech researchers to cross-border export of meth from Czechia.
7Oslo, NOThe Nordic outlier: historically the highest meth loads outside Czechia/Slovakia, with pronounced year-to-year fluctuation reflecting supply waves.
8Vilnius, LTThe Baltic signal: measurable meth loads in a region otherwise dominated by amphetamine and fentanyl analogues.
9Madrid / eastern Spain, ESSpanish cities register the western edge of Europe's meth belt, alongside detections in the Netherlands and Belgium.
10Istanbul, TRTürkiye's meth loads have risen sharply — analysts link it to Afghan-produced meth moving west along the heroin routes through Iran.

Also worth knowing

Sixteen monitored European cities reported no meth detection at all in 2025 — the drug remains remarkably regional. EUDA's own comparison: city-level meth loads in Australia, Canada and the United States exceed every European city.

Methodology

Methodology: SCORE/EUDA wastewater analysis (sampling March–May 2025, published March 2026) — daily methamphetamine mass loads per 1,000 population, supplemented by EUDA trend reporting. This is the only region where the ranking is directly measured rather than triangulated.
