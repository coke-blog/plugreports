Top 10 Australian cities with the most vaping

Vaping in Australia — a prescription-only legal market drowned by a billion-dollar black market, from Sydney's vape-shop raids to the Gold Coast's teen crisis, per ABF seizure data.

13.2Millicit vapes seized at the Australian border in the 2025–26 financial year — up 112% on the year before (ABF)

Rx-onlyAustralia is the only Western country where nicotine vapes are legally a pharmacy-only, therapeutic product

How this ranking works

Australia is the world's strangest vape market: since October 2021, buying a nicotine vape legally has required a doctor's prescription, and since 2024 vapes are supposed to be sold only in pharmacies as quitting aids — plain packaging, three flavors. In practice, a black market worth billions flooded the country with flavored disposables sold openly in convenience stores, and enforcement statistics now tell the story: 13.2 million vapes seized at the border in 2025–26 alone. No official city-level use data exists, so this ranking combines seizure geography, state health surveys and market reporting. Treat it as a map of where the illicit market is biggest.

Read this firstNo country anywhere measures vaping use per city directly. This ranking combines the strongest available proxies — wastewater analysis, forensic-lab reporting, treatment admissions and police seizures — and is directional, not a precise league table. Positions in the middle of each list should be read as a band.
#CityWhy it ranks here
1Sydney, NSWThe epicenter of the black market: NSW health surveys found the country's highest teen vaping rates, Sydney's convenience-store vape trade operated near-openly for years, and the state now runs the most retail prosecutions — plus the most firebombings, as organized crime fights over the trade.
2Melbourne, VICVictoria's illicit-tobacco and vape war is the country's most violent — dozens of arson attacks on shops since 2023 — because Melbourne is the second-biggest market and the first to get a state licensing scheme.
3Brisbane / Gold Coast, QLDQueensland's southeast is Australia's teen-vaping flashpoint: school surveys and principals' reporting made the Gold Coast the national symbol of the youth crisis, and border-force seizures at the Port of Brisbane are among the largest.
4Perth, WAWA's isolation didn't protect it — Perth's black market is supplied through Fremantle port, and state surveys show adult vaping rates tracking the east coast.
5Adelaide, SAA smaller but well-documented market; SA Health's surveys captured some of the earliest steep rises in young-adult vaping in the country.
6Darwin, NTThe Territory's frontier retail (vapes sold from car yards and grocers, per ABC reporting of ABF raids) makes Darwin's per-capita illicit trade arguably the country's most brazen.
7Canberra, ACTThe capital's market is small and young — ANU's campus surveys made national news — but enforcement is the country's tightest.
8Hobart, TASTasmania's vape trade follows its older nicotine patterns: smaller volumes, high per-capita nicotine use in wastewater, and a steady convenience-store market.
9Newcastle / Wollongong, NSWSydney's satellite cities share NSW's top-tier survey rates and its enforcement crackdown.
10Cairns / Townsville, QLDRegional Queensland's youth vaping rates in state school surveys match the southeast — the black market reached the far north fast.

Also worth knowing

The policy experiment to watch: Australia's 2024–25 reforms (pharmacy-only sales, then prescription-free-but-pharmacist-mediated supply for adults) are the most restrictive legal framework in the Western world, running against a black market that seizure data suggests is still growing. Whether the illicit market shrinks or the legal one bends is the defining question of the next few years — and the arson wars in Melbourne and Sydney show what's at stake.

Methodology

Methodology: Australian Border Force seizure statistics (FY2024–26), Illicit Tobacco and E-cigarette Commissioner reporting, state health-department surveys, and ACIC wastewater nicotine data. No city-level use measurement exists; ranks are estimated from seizure geography and survey patterns. Rankings are directional.
