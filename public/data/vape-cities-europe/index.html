Top 10 European cities with the most vaping

Europe's vaping capitals — Britain vapes more than anyone else in Europe, with London, Manchester and Glasgow leading 5.5 million UK vapers, per ASH and national survey data.

How this ranking works

Europe's vaping map is lopsided: the United Kingdom embraced e-cigarettes as a smoking-cessation tool and built the continent's biggest market by far — 5.5 million vapers, roughly one in ten adults — while most of the EU regulates vaping as a consumer product with tighter nicotine caps (20 mg/mL under the TPD) and, increasingly, flavor and disposable bans. No European agency measures vaping per city, so this ranking combines ASH's GB surveys, national tobacco surveys, and market data. UK cities dominate by volume; continental entries reflect each country's survey leaders. Treat positions as a band.

Read this firstNo country anywhere measures vaping use per city directly. This ranking combines the strongest available proxies — wastewater analysis, forensic-lab reporting, treatment admissions and police seizures — and is directional, not a precise league table. Positions in the middle of each list should be read as a band.
#CityWhy it ranks here
1London, UKEurope's largest vape market in absolute terms: London's share of Britain's 5.5 million vapers, thousands of dedicated vape shops, and the country's most visible disposable-litter problem before the June 2025 ban.
2Manchester, UKThe Northwest consistently posts some of England's highest vaping (and smoking) rates in ASH and ONS breakdowns — Manchester's young demographics amplify it.
3Birmingham, UKBritain's second city tracks Manchester closely; the Midlands' above-average smoking rates have converted into above-average vaping as smokers switch.
4Glasgow, UKScotland's largest city: Scottish health surveys show some of the UK's highest smoking and vaping rates, and Glasgow's vape-shop density is the highest in Scotland.
5Liverpool, UKMerseyside rounds out England's high-prevalence northwest cluster; trading-standards raids on illicit vapes here are among the most frequent in the country.
6Paris, FRFrance counts several million vapers (OFDT surveys put experimentation near half of young adults), and Paris is the continent's largest non-UK market — though France banned disposables in February 2025.
7Berlin, DEGermany's vape market is the EU's second largest; Berlin's nightlife districts drove the disposable boom, and German youth surveys showed some of Europe's fastest rises pre-2024.
8Prague, CZCzechia's liberal nicotine market (plus a booming nicotine-pouch scene) makes Prague Central Europe's vaping capital by survey rates.
9Warsaw, PLPoland's large market skews young — Polish teen vaping rates have ranked among the EU's highest in ESPAD school surveys — and Warsaw concentrates it.
10Madrid, ESSpain's market grew fast on disposables among teenagers (a major policy flashpoint in 2023–24), with Madrid and Barcelona as the twin hubs.

Also worth knowing

Just outside: Brussels (Belgium banned disposables in January 2025 — a first in the EU), Amsterdam, Rome and Stockholm (where snus and nicotine pouches suppress vaping). The continental trend is restriction — disposable bans in Belgium, France and the UK, flavor limits spreading — while Britain alone still officially promotes vaping as a quit-smoking tool, even as it tightens youth access.

Methodology

Methodology: ASH Smokefree GB adult and youth surveys (2024–2026), ONS adult smoking/vaping breakdowns, OFDT (France), national tobacco surveys, and market reporting. No city-level measurement exists; city ranks within countries are estimated from regional survey patterns and retail data. Rankings are directional.
