Top 10 Australian cities with the most methamphetamine use

Australia records the highest city-level meth loads in the world — and the worst of it is in regional areas, not the capitals. Ranked from ACIC wastewater data.

How this ranking works

Australia's National Wastewater Drug Monitoring Program, run by the Australian Criminal Intelligence Commission, is one of the most rigorous drug-surveillance systems anywhere — 61 treatment plants covering 57% of the population. Its headline finding turns the usual assumption upside down: in Australia, meth use is higher in regional areas than in capital cities. 'Ice' is Australia's most destructive illicit drug by spending, and regional mining towns, ports and agricultural centers — not Sydney or Melbourne — carry the heaviest per-capita loads.

Read this firstNo country anywhere measures meth use per city directly. This ranking combines the strongest available proxies — wastewater analysis, forensic-lab reporting, treatment admissions and police seizures — and is directional, not a precise league table. Positions in the middle of each list should be read as a band.
#CityWhy it ranks here
1Perth, WAWestern Australia records the highest meth loads of any Australian region; Perth's market is fed by direct maritime import from Asia, and WA consistently tops ACIC's per-capital-city meth ranking.
2Darwin, NTSmall population, extreme per-capita loads — Darwin repeatedly posts among the highest meth consumption figures per person in the national program.
3Regional WA (Kalgoorlie, Geraldton, Bunbury)The mining-and-ports belt records the heaviest regional meth loads in the country; fly-in-fly-out work patterns and port access drive supply.
4Adelaide, SASA Health and ACIC data place Adelaide among the highest capital-city meth markets, with strong links to Victorian supply networks.
5Brisbane / Gold Coast, QLDQueensland's southeast corridor — high absolute consumption and a documented role as an import and redistribution gateway.
6Sydney, NSWLargest absolute market; per-capita figures are lower than Perth or Darwin but the total tonnage flowing through the city is the country's biggest.
7Melbourne, VICSecond-largest absolute market; Victoria's wastewater shows high and stable meth loads with regional hotspots (Shepparton, Mildura).
8Regional Victoria (Shepparton, Mildura) / Regional NSW (Dubbo, Wagga Wagga)Country cities consistently out-consume their capitals per person; ice is the defining drug of regional Australia.
9Hobart, TASTasmania's isolation keeps loads moderate but the state's regional towns post disproportionate figures relative to population.
10Canberra, ACTThe national capital records the lowest meth loads of any Australian jurisdiction — though ACIC notes use rose here too in the latest round.

Also worth knowing

The single most important fact in Australian meth data: regional Australia uses more meth per person than the capitals — the reverse of the cocaine pattern, which is city-dominated. National meth consumption hit a record 12.8 tonnes in the 2023–24 monitoring year.

Methodology

Methodology: ACIC National Wastewater Drug Monitoring Program (2023–24 reporting year, 61 sites covering 57% of population) — methamphetamine mass loads by site, combined with state health data. Rankings combine per-capita loads with absolute market size.
