Top 10 Canadian cities with the most methamphetamine use

Where meth hits hardest in Canada — prairie cities dominate, per wastewater data, RCMP/ALERT seizures and provincial treatment reports.

How this ranking works

Canada's meth geography is different from America's: the epicenter is not the West Coast but the Prairie provinces. Wastewater monitoring (Canadian cities participate in the international SCORE study), provincial treatment data and police seizure reports all point the same way — Manitoba, Saskatchewan and Alberta carry the heaviest per-capita meth load, with British Columbia and Ontario as secondary markets fed through prairie distribution networks.

Read this firstNo country anywhere measures meth use per city directly. This ranking combines the strongest available proxies — wastewater analysis, forensic-lab reporting, treatment admissions and police seizures — and is directional, not a precise league table. Positions in the middle of each list should be read as a band.
#CityWhy it ranks here
1Winnipeg, MBConsistently Canada's meth capital across every proxy: among the highest meth loads in Canadian wastewater data, the highest per-capita police seizure rates, and a treatment system where meth is the leading stimulant referral. Winnipeg is also named as a source node in interprovincial trafficking cases (a 2025 ALERT case traced a 20 kg shipment from Winnipeg to Edmonton).
2Edmonton, ABAlberta's northern hub; RCMP and ALERT report Edmonton as a primary meth distribution center, and the city records some of the province's highest overdose and seizure figures. CBSA seized 279 kg of meth in Alberta in 2025 — most destined for the Edmonton–Calgary corridor.
3Calgary, ABClose behind Edmonton on every indicator; the two cities function as one market split by 300 km of highway.
4Vancouver, BCLower Mainland meth is tied to organized crime groups and the port; Vancouver participates in SCORE wastewater monitoring and shows significant meth loads, though fentanyl dominates its overdose statistics.
5Saskatoon, SKSaskatchewan's largest city and its meth distribution hub; provincial data shows meth-involved offences and seizures far above the national rate.
6Regina, SKMatches Saskatoon on per-capita indicators; the two prairie cities trade the provincial top spot year to year.
7Toronto, ONCanada's largest city by consumption volume, though lower per capita than the prairies; meth is the fastest-growing stimulant in Ontario treatment data.
8Windsor, ON / London, ONSouthern Ontario corridor cities absorbing mid-west US supply across the Detroit border.
9Halifax, NSAtlantic Canada's meth entry point, fed by port traffic and highway corridors from Montreal.
10Montreal, QCQuebec's meth market is smaller but growing — SQ reporting shows rising seizures, often linked to Hells Angels distribution networks.

Also worth knowing

Territorial note: Whitehorse, Yellowknife and Iqaluit report low absolute numbers but among the highest per-capita meth-related harm in Canadian surveillance, driven by limited treatment access.

Methodology

Methodology: composite of Statistics Canada police-reported drug offences by CMA, CBSA and RCMP/ALERT seizure reporting (2024–26), provincial treatment admissions, and SCORE wastewater data where Canadian cities participate. Rankings are directional.

Sources

  • Statistics Canada police-reported crime statistics
  • CBSA seizure reporting 2025–26
  • ALERT / RCMP releases
  • SCORE wastewater study (EUDA)
  • Provincial treatment admissions (MB, AB, SK, BC)
