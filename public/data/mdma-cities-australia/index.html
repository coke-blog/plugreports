Top 10 Australian cities with the most MDMA use

Sydney and Melbourne lead Australia's MDMA map — the ACIC wastewater program tracks the festival-driven market from Byron Bay to Perth.

How this ranking works

Australia has excellent MDMA data: the ACIC's National Wastewater Drug Monitoring Program measures MDMA at ~60 sites covering most of the population. The pattern is the mirror image of meth: MDMA is a capital-city, weekend-and-festival drug, with Sydney and Melbourne leading on absolute consumption and summer spikes around the festival calendar. Australia is also a high-price market — pills and caps here cost 3–5× European street prices.

Read this firstNo country anywhere measures mdma use per city directly. This ranking combines the strongest available proxies — wastewater analysis, forensic-lab reporting, treatment admissions and police seizures — and is directional, not a precise league table. Positions in the middle of each list should be read as a band.
#CityWhy it ranks here
1Sydney, NSWAustralia's largest MDMA market — the biggest club economy and festival calendar (and historically its strictest policing, which shaped the pill-testing debate).
2Melbourne, VICThe live-music capital — Melbourne's club and warehouse scene keeps it level with Sydney in wastewater terms.
3Brisbane, QLDQueensland's hub — steady year-round club demand plus the festival corridor down to Byron Bay.
4Perth, WAThe most isolated big city in the world still posts high per-capita MDMA loads — a young, cashed-up market.
5Gold Coast, QLDTourism + nightlife + Schoolies week: the Gold Coast's MDMA indicators spike hard in November–December.
6Byron Bay / Northern Rivers, NSWSmall population, huge festival footprint (Splendour in the Grass region) — per-capita indicators are among Australia's highest.
7Adelaide, SAA steady mid-table market with strong festival-season spikes.
8Canberra, ACTHome of Australia's first fixed pill-testing trial (CanTEST) — the data from it shows just how strong and mis-sold the local supply is.
9Hobart, TASSmall but growing market — Tasmania's indicators have risen every recent reporting period.
10Darwin, NTThe smallest capital — MDMA indicators are modest here; Darwin's stimulant problem is meth, not MDMA.

Also worth knowing

The Australian quirk: MDMA here is expensive (~$25–$35 per pill/cap) and frequently mis-sold — CanTEST pill-testing has found everything from high-dose MDMA to cathinones sold as 'molly'. Festival season (December–March) is when the hospital presentations spike.

Methodology

Methodology: ACIC National Wastewater Drug Monitoring Program (Year 9+ reporting), state survey data, seizure reporting, CanTEST/The Loop pill-testing results. Rankings are directional.
