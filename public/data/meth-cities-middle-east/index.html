Meth in the Middle East: the cities where it concentrates

The Middle East runs on Captagon — but methamphetamine is the region's other stimulant epidemic, centered on Iran and Israel with Afghan supply flooding east.

959meth labs dismantled in Iran in a single 3-year period (2010–13) — historically the region's epicenter

Top-2Iran and Israel are the region's two established meth consumer markets

How this ranking works

Any honest Middle East meth ranking starts with a caveat: this is a Captagon region. The Levant's stimulant of choice is the amphetamine pill made in Syria, and most countries here publish almost no city-level drug data at all. But methamphetamine — not Captagon — has carved out two real consumer markets: Iran, the region's historic meth producer, and Israel, where meth (locally 'metz') became the dominant hard drug of the 2010s. Around them, Afghan meth production is now flooding east into Iran, Türkiye and the Gulf.

Read this firstNo country anywhere measures meth use per city directly. This ranking combines the strongest available proxies — wastewater analysis, forensic-lab reporting, treatment admissions and police seizures — and is directional, not a precise league table. Positions in the middle of each list should be read as a band.
#CityWhy it ranks here
1Tehran, IranThe region's meth capital by every historical measure: the largest consumer base, thousands of dismantled labs, and a well-documented rise in regular use and meth-associated psychosis. Iranian national survey data put past-year meth use at roughly 0.7% of adults — hundreds of thousands of users, concentrated in the capital.
2Mashhad / eastern IranEastern provinces bear the Afghan wave: 77.5% of people who inject drugs in eastern Iran test positive for meth, versus 14.1% in the west — a direct consequence of Afghan meth moving across the border.
3Tel Aviv / central Israel, ILIsrael's largest meth market; Israeli police reporting has repeatedly identified meth as the country's most consumed hard drug, with a supply chain stretching to Mexico and Europe.
4Beersheba / southern Israel, ILIsrael's second-tier meth hub, serving the Negev and the country's largest periphery city.
5Istanbul, TürkiyeTürkiye is a transit-and-emerging-consumer country: SCORE wastewater already detects rising meth loads in Istanbul, fed by the same Afghan pipeline that serves eastern Iran.
6Dubai / UAE, AEThe Gulf's entry point: repeated record meth seizures at UAE ports and airports indicate a real — if hidden — consumer market among the region's largest expatriate population.
7Riyadh / Jeddah, SASaudi Arabia reports large ATS seizures annually; harsh enforcement means prevalence data is close to nonexistent, but seizure volumes point to Jeddah's port and Riyadh's market.
8Amman, JOJordan sits between the Captagon belt and the Gulf; meth detections are rising in national reporting alongside the Captagon flood.

Also worth knowing

Data honesty: outside Iran and Israel, no Middle Eastern country publishes city-level prevalence. Rankings 3–8 are inferred from seizure geography, wastewater signals and treatment reporting, and are more uncertain than any other region on this site.

Methodology

Methodology: triangulation of UNODC and WHO-EMRO reporting, Iranian national and regional studies (including PWID cohort data), Israeli Anti-Drug Authority / police statistics, and SCORE wastewater signals where available. High uncertainty outside Iran and Israel.
