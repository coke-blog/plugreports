Benzodiacepinas

Alprazolam (Xanax 2 mg)

Street names: Xanax, Bars, Pfizer LongBars, Zans, Footballs

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • Alivio rápido de la ansiedad
  • Calma sedante
  • Relajación muscular

⚠Key risks

  • Dependencia en semanas; abstinencia brutal (riesgo de convulsiones)
  • Las barras falsificadas contienen fentanilo o bromazolam
  • Amnesia y conductas riesgosas

✚Overdose signs

  • Somnolencia extrema
  • Habla arrastrada
  • Respiración lenta — mortal con opioides o alcohol

?Frequently asked questions

What does Alprazolam (Xanax 2 mg) look like?

Barras blancas de 2 mg (largas, ranuradas); tabletas azules/blancas/amarillas según la dosis

What are the signs of a Alprazolam (Xanax 2 mg) overdose?

Somnolencia extrema; Habla arrastrada; Respiración lenta — mortal con opioides o alcohol Call emergency services immediately.

How addictive is it?

Dependencia en semanas; abstinencia brutal (riesgo de convulsiones) Withdrawal can be life-threatening — medical tapering is essential.
