Fentanyl: the numbers nobody tells you

Updated 2026-09-07Reviewed against NIDA / DEA / EMCDDA sources Suggest a correction

Fentanyl is a legitimate medicine — 50–100x morphine's potency, used for surgical pain and end-of-life care. Illegally manufactured fentanyl is the same molecule made in clandestine labs, pressed into fake pills that mimic oxycodone, Xanax, Adderall — even candy-colored tablets.

The arithmetic that kills

  • A lethal dose is 2 mg; a $20 bag can hold hundreds of lethal doses — street mixes are eyeballed, not scaled.
  • Pills are pressed, not dosed: one pill in a batch can be 10x another.
  • Fentanyl builds dependence fast: 3–5 days of daily use creates physical dependence.

The reversal arithmetic that saves

Naloxone displaces fentanyl from opioid receptors. Because fentanyl binds hard, one spray may reverse for only 30–90 minutes while fentanyl outlasts it — the person can re-overdose. Protocol: give naloxone, call 911, and STAY — even after they wake up.

Unresponsive, pinpoint pupils, slow/gurgling breathingCall 911 (Good Samaritan laws protect you in most places)
