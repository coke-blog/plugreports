GHK-Cu (Copper Peptide)

Street names: Copper tripeptide, GHK-Cu

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • Skin repair and hair-growth claims (topical evidence reasonable)
  • Injectable form marketed for systemic 'regeneration' — no human evidence

⚠Key risks

  • Injectable copper peptide: zero human safety data
  • Blue discoloration at injection sites (cosmetic)
  • Research-chemical purity issues

✚Overdose signs

  • Unknown (injectable)
  • Injection-site reaction
  • Skin discoloration
