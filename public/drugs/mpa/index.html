Methiopropamine (MPA)

Street names: MPA, Blow (UK, misleadingly)

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • Mild-moderate stimulation, focus, long duration

⚠Key risks

  • Thiophene ring — unknown long-term metabolism
  • Class-typical heart strain

✚Overdose signs

  • Racing heart
  • Insomnia mania
  • Hypertension
