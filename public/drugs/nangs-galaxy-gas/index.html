Galaxy Gas (Flavored Nitrous)

Street names: Galaxy Gas, Whipped cream chargers (large), Flavored nos, 640g tanks

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • Same as nitrous — brief euphoria, dissociation, giggling

⚠Key risks

  • Same B12-destruction and nerve damage as whippets — larger canisters make heavy use CHEAPER and easier
  • Flavored versions marketed AT young people — pastel colors, dessert flavors
  • Teen social-media trend — 'Galaxy Gas challenge'
  • Oxygen deprivation at heavy sessions
  • B12 nerve damage in heavy teen users documented (weak legs, numbness)

✚Overdose signs

  • Blue lips (oxygen deprivation)
  • Unconsciousness
  • Seizures
  • B12 deficiency neuropathy (chronic)
