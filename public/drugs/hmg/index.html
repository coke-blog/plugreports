HMG (Human Menopausal Gonadotropin)

Street names: HMG, Menopur-style, GMH HP

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • FSH + LH activity — the fertility-restoration step beyond hCG
  • Used when hCG alone doesn't restore sperm production

⚠Key risks

  • Expensive and heavily faked
  • Injection-site reactions
  • Overstimulation risk at fertility-clinic doses

✚Overdose signs

  • No acute overdose
  • Injection-site infection
  • Abdominal discomfort
