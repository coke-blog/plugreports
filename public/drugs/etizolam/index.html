Benzodiazepines

Etizolam

Street names: Etiz, Etilaam, Etizest, Passionflower (branded)

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • Fast anxiety relief and sedation, short-acting

⚠Key risks

  • Extremely popular RC benzo — dependence sneaks up fast
  • Fake 'Xanax' bars often contain etizolam or stronger analogues

✚Overdose signs

  • Sedation
  • Amnesia
  • Respiratory depression when mixed

📋Quick facts

Also known asEtiz, Etilaam, Etizest, Passionflower (branded)
CategoryBenzodiazepines
Schedule / classNot federally scheduled (US) — controlled in AL/AR/FL/OH/TX/VA etc.; banned UK 2017
AppearancePink/blue 1 mg tablets or pink round pellets ('pellets'); blotters
Street price~$0.50–$3 per 1 mg tablet/pellet
Legal statusLegal gray zone federally (US); illegal in UK, Germany, several US states; Rx in Italy/India/Japan
Last updated2026-09-12
Sources: EMCDDA, TripSit, Erowid
