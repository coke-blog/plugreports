Melanotan II

Street names: MT2, Melanotan, The Barbie drug

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • Skin tanning without sun (melanocortin receptor activation)
  • Libido and appetite-suppression side effects

⚠Key risks

  • Unapproved — regulators (FDA/TGA/MHRA) have issued warnings and seizures
  • 'Melanotan rage' — documented mood/aggression changes
  • Moles darkening and new nevi — dermatologists flag melanoma-monitoring problems
  • Nausea, flushing, blood-pressure spikes after injection; contaminated vials common

✚Overdose signs

  • Severe nausea, vomiting
  • Blood pressure spikes, fainting
  • Skin changes needing dermatology review
