CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin Stacks

Street names: CJC, Ipamorelin, GH stack, The peptide clinic combo

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • GH-releasing peptide combo: marketed for fat loss, recovery, 'anti-aging' — modest real GH elevation

⚠Key risks

  • Zero long-term human safety data; cancer-growth theoretical concern with chronic GH elevation
  • Gray 'clinics' operating outside medical-board norms; compounding-pharmacy quality variance
  • Water retention, carpal-tunnel symptoms, glucose intolerance
  • The wellness-industry pipeline: sold with fake 'growth hormone panels'
  • CJC with DAC has long half-life — accumulation

✚Overdose signs

  • Severe water retention, joint pain
  • Blood sugar elevation
  • Injection-site infection (grey vials)
