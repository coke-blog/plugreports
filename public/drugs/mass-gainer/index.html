Mass Gainer Supplements

Street names: mass gainer, weight gainer, serious mass, gainer shake, hard gainer

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • Convenient calorie surplus
  • Weight gain when total intake rises
  • Post-workout carbohydrate and protein

⚠Key risks

  • Mostly cheap sugar — many gainers are closer to milkshake mix than sports nutrition
  • Spiked products (undisclosed steroids or stimulants) recur in supplement testing
  • Rapid fat gain rather than muscle when training is light
  • Digestive distress from huge servings
  • Not third-party tested in many cases

✚Overdose signs

  • Not acutely toxic; chronic overuse drives fat gain, blood-sugar problems, and digestive issues
