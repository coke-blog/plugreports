Refrigerant / Freon Huffing

Street names: Freon, AC coolant, R-22, Refrigerant gas

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • Brief euphoria, disorientation
  • Floating sensation

⚠Key risks

  • EXTREMELY dangerous — refrigerant gases (difluoroethane, tetrafluoroethane) cause sudden cardiac death at high rates
  • Frostbite to airways
  • Oxygen displacement — suffocation
  • Refrigerant is heavier than air — pools in lungs
  • Often fatal on FIRST use

✚Overdose signs

  • Sudden cardiac arrest
  • Seizures
  • Blue lips, unconsciousness
  • Death within minutes
