Etonitazepyne (NPE)

Street names: NPE, Etonitazepyne HCl, 'Neo' nitazene

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • Potent nitazene opioid — euphoria, warmth and heavy sedation like other members of the class, at a fraction of the weight
  • Effects last 4–8 hours, longer than fentanyl — and so does the overdose risk window
  • Tolerance builds immediately; doses escalate within days

⚠Key risks

  • The leading 'next-generation' nitazene in 2025–26 seizure and toxicology reports — positioned to replace isotonitazene as the class's flagship
  • Dosed in micrograms: the line between a dose and a fatal overdose is invisible without laboratory equipment
  • Counterfeit pills containing NPE are sold as 30 mg oxycodone — users have no idea they're taking a drug orders of magnitude stronger
  • Naloxone still works, but high-potency overdoses often need repeated doses and rescue breathing while it catches up
  • Dependence and withdrawal hit hard and fast — full opioid dependence within weeks of regular use

✚Overdose signs

  • Unrousable, blue lips or fingertips
  • Breathing fewer than 8 times a minute, or not at all
  • Pinpoint pupils with gurgling or snoring sounds — give naloxone, call emergency services, start rescue breathing
