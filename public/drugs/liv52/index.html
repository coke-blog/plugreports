LIV 52 (herbal liver supplement)

Street names: Liv 52, Liv52 DS, Himalaya Liv 52

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • The most-sold 'liver support' supplement in the steroid world
  • Some evidence for alcohol-related liver enzyme improvement

⚠Key risks

  • NOT protection — no evidence it prevents steroid or oral-AAS liver harm; users treat it as a license to abuse orals
  • Black nightshade content raises theoretical safety questions at high dose
  • Belief in it delays actual liver monitoring (blood tests)

✚Overdose signs

  • No acute overdose
  • GI upset
