Crack Cocaine

Street names: Crack, Rock, Stones, Base, Freebase

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • Intense but very short euphoria (5–15 minutes), energy and confidence
  • Rapid crash: irritability, exhaustion, intense craving for another hit
  • Smoked route hits the brain in seconds — one of the most reinforcing drug experiences known

⚠Key risks

  • The short high creates a compulsive redosing loop that drives binge patterns and rapid dependence
  • Crack lung: chest pain, cough and lung bleeding (eosinophilic pneumonitis) after smoking sessions
  • Chipped/burned lips and pipe-sharing spread MRSA, hepatitis C and other infections
  • Heart attack, dangerous arrhythmias and stroke — risk is real even in young users
  • Paranoia, aggression and stimulant psychosis with binges; malnutrition and sleep loss compound harm
  • Same supply risk as powder cocaine: cuts and, increasingly, fentanyl contamination

✚Overdose signs

  • Chest pain, pounding or irregular heartbeat
  • Seizures; extremely high body temperature
  • Severe agitation, paranoia or hallucinations
  • Stroke signs: one-sided weakness, slurred speech, facial droop
