DOx Family (DOB, DOC, DOI)

Street names: DOB, DOC, DOI, The 24-hour acid

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • 24–36 HOUR amphetamine-psychedelic: energy + visuals that just do not stop
  • DOM (STP) is the family patriarch

⚠Key risks

  • THE TIME TRAP: sold as LSD-sized doses of LSD — a full DOB dose is tiny, and users take 'one more tab' → 36-hour amphetamine psychosis, hospitalizations
  • Strong vasoconstriction: cold extremities, BP danger
  • Cardiac strain over the marathon duration
  • If a blotter takes 3+ hours to work, you may have a DOx — do not redose

✚Overdose signs

  • Racing irregular heart for hours
  • Severe agitation, hallucinations
  • Overheating
