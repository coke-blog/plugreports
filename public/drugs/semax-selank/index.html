Semax & Selank (Russian Nootropic Peptides)

Street names: Semax, Selank, N-Acetyl Semax, ACTH fragment, Tuftsin

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • Semax: ACTH(4-10) analog — focus/neuroprotection claims; Selank: tuftsin analog — anxiolytic without sedation. The CIS 'smart nasal sprays'

⚠Key risks

  • Russian clinical data exists but Western replication is thin
  • Grey-market nasal sprays: sterility and dosing unverified
  • Effect sizes modest; expectation hype large
  • Chronic melanocortin stimulation — theoretical unknowns

✚Overdose signs

  • Nasal irritation
  • Headache, agitation at high doses
