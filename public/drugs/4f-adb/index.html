Cannabinoids

4F-ADB

Street names: 4F-MDMB-BUTINACA

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • Cannabis-like high but far more intense — sedation, confusion, anxiety

⚠Key risks

  • Associated with 'Spice zombies' — catatonia, seizures
  • Unpredictable potency sprayed unevenly
  • Kidney damage, heart rhythm problems

✚Overdose signs

  • Severe agitation, psychosis
  • Seizures
  • Racing heart, vomiting
