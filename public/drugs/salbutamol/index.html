Salbutamol (Albuterol/Ventolin)

Street names: Albuterol, Ventolin, Asthalin, ProAir

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • Instant bronchodilation — relieves asthma attacks in minutes
  • Grey-market use: stimulant-adjacent 'cutting' aid and clenbuterol alternative

⚠Key risks

  • Tremor, racing heart at high doses
  • Potassium drop (hypokalemia) — dangerous with cardiac conditions
  • Counterfeit inhalers deliver wrong doses — genuinely dangerous for asthmatics
  • Used by bodybuilders as a 'safer clen' — the margin isn't as wide as assumed

✚Overdose signs

  • Racing irregular heart, tremor
  • Chest pain — emergency
  • Severe headache (BP)
