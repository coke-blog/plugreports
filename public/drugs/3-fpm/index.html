Stimulants

3-FPM (3-Fluorophenmetrazine)

Street names: 3-FPM, PAL-593

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • Functional stimulation: focus, mild euphoria
  • Less euphoric than amphetamine

⚠Key risks

  • Vasoconstriction reports — cold extremities
  • Binge redosing
  • Little long-term data

✚Overdose signs

  • Vasoconstriction, cold extremities
  • Racing heart
