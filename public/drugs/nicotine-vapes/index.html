Nicotine E-Cigarettes & Disposables

Street names: Vapes, E-cigs, Elf Bar, Esco Bar, Puff Bar, JUUL

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • Fast nicotine: the same 10-second brain hit as cigarettes
  • Smooth high-nicotine salts — easy to inhale deeply, easy to overuse

⚠Key risks

  • THE teen nicotine epidemic — 2023-2025: disposables with up to 5% (50 mg/mL) nicotine salt, designed for addiction
  • Adolescent brain development disruption — attention, learning, mood regulation, impulse control
  • One disposable = a pack+ of cigarettes in nicotine
  • EVALI-style lung injuries tied to black-market THC carts; nicotine vapes carry their own unknowns
  • Gateway pattern: teens who vape are 3–4× more likely to start smoking
  • Battery explosions, nicotine poisoning in toddlers who eat pods

✚Overdose signs

  • Nicotine poisoning: nausea, vomiting, dizziness, sweating
  • Racing heart, tremor
  • Severe: seizures, respiratory distress — emergency (especially children)
