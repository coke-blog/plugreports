Mirtazapine (Remeron)

Street names: Remeron, Mirtaz, Zispin

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • Sedation and appetite increase at low doses; antidepressant at 30–45 mg
  • Popular off-label sleep aid

⚠Key risks

  • Not euphoric but dependence-forming as a sleep crutch
  • Weight gain, intense hunger (some abuse for appetite)
  • Withdrawal: nausea, insomnia, anxiety, 'electric shocks'
  • Serotonin syndrome risk combined with other serotonergic drugs
  • Morning impairment: driving, falls

✚Overdose signs

  • Severe sedation
  • Confusion
  • Heart rhythm changes (rare)
