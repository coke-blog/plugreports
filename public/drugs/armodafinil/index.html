Armodafinil (Nuvigil)

Street names: Nuvigil, R-modafinil, Waklert (generic)

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • The R-enantiomer of modafinil — longer, smoother wakefulness (12–15 h)

⚠Key risks

  • Same class as modafinil: headache, anxiety, insomnia
  • Stevens-Johnson syndrome warning (class, rare)
  • BP elevation; heart strain with caffeine stacks
  • Dependence psychological; crash on cessation
  • Grey-market 'Waklert/Artvigil' tablets — content variance

✚Overdose signs

  • Agitation, insomnia
  • Racing heart, chest pain
