Potassium Cyanide (KCN)

Street names: KCN, Cyanide salts

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • NONE — this is not a recreational drug. Blocks cellular oxygen use within minutes

⚠Key risks

  • Lethal dose ~200–300 mg — death within minutes to an hour
  • No antidote available outside hospital; hydrogen cyanide gas from acid contact

✚Overdose signs

  • All exposure is poisoning: gasping, seizures, collapse, cardiac arrest
