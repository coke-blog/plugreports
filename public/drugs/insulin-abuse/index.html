Insulin (Bodybuilding Abuse)

Street names: R, N, Humalog, Novorapid, Lantus, The most dangerous gym drug

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • Drives glucose into muscle — bodybuilders stack it with HGH/steroids for growth; 'the most anabolic hormone' gym lore

⚠Key risks

  • HYPOGLYCEMIC DEATH is the signature: misdosed or mistimed insulin drops blood sugar — brain damage or death within hours; bodybuilder deaths documented
  • No margin: a few units' error, missed meal, or unexpected cardio can kill
  • Often sourced OTC without ANY medical supervision — no glucose meter discipline
  • The gym-bro 'start at 2 IU' forum dosing has hospitalized thousands
  • Unlike steroids, insulin is not a 'try it and see' drug — the first mistake can be the last

✚Overdose signs

  • Shakiness, sweating, confusion (hypoglycemia)
  • Seizures, unconsciousness
  • Death if untreated — sugar/juice/glucagon immediately if conscious
