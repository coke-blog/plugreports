Etonitazene

Street names: Etazene, EZ

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • Intense opioid euphoria at microgram doses

⚠Key risks

  • ~10–25× fentanyl potency
  • Active at sub-milligram doses — nearly impossible to dose safely

✚Overdose signs

  • Immediate respiratory depression
  • Unresponsiveness
  • Pinpoint pupils
