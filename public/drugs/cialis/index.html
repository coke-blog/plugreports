Tadalafil (Cialis)

Street names: Cialis, Tadalis, Weekend pill

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • 36-hour erectile enhancement

⚠Key risks

  • Counterfeit ED pills are a major fentanyl/adulterant channel in some regions
  • Dangerous blood pressure drop with nitrates

✚Overdose signs

  • Prolonged erection (>4 h — medical emergency)
  • Chest pain, dizziness
