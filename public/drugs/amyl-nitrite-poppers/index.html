Amyl Nitrite Poppers

Street names: Poppers, Rush, Jungle Juice, Amyl, Liquid gold

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • 10–30 second head rush, warmth, euphoria
  • Muscle relaxation, skin sensitivity

⚠Key risks

  • FATAL blood-pressure crash with Viagra/Cialis (PDE5 inhibitors)
  • Methemoglobinemia — blood stops carrying oxygen (blue lips, breathlessness)
  • Poppers maculopathy — permanent vision damage
  • Skin burns, headache, fainting
  • Rebound blood-pressure spikes

✚Overdose signs

  • Blue/gray lips and skin
  • Fainting, extreme dizziness
  • Racing heart then collapse
  • Breathing difficulty
