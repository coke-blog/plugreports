Cigars & Cigarillos

Street names: Cigars, Cigarillos, Backwoods, Swisher Sweets, Blunts

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • Nicotine — cigars can contain 5–20× a cigarette's nicotine; absorbed through mouth lining even without inhaling

⚠Key risks

  • Cigar smoking causes the same cancers as cigarettes — lung, oral, esophageal, laryngeal — even if you don't inhale
  • Single cigars can deliver a pack's nicotine
  • THE BLUNT WRAP PIPELINE — cigarillos exist in youth cannabis culture as blunt wraps; sweet flavors (wine, strawberry) marketed at young buyers
  • Secondhand cigar smoke more toxic than cigarette smoke
  • Lower taxes = cheaper entry point for teens

✚Overdose signs

  • Nicotine poisoning: nausea, dizziness
  • Palpitations
