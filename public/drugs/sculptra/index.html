Poly-L-lactic Acid (Sculptra)

Street names: Sculptra, PLLA, Liquid facelift, New-Fill

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • Stimulates your own collagen over months — gradual, natural-looking volume lasting ~2 years

⚠Key risks

  • NO HYALURONIDASE ANTIDOTE: unlike HA fillers, PLLA cannot be dissolved — overfill, lumps (nodules), and migration are semi-permanent
  • Nodule formation weeks later requires steroid or surgical correction
  • Vascular occlusion same as any injectable
  • Counterfeit 'Sculptra' from gray market
  • The 'can't undo it' factor makes injector skill even more critical

✚Overdose signs

  • Firm nodules weeks post-injection
  • Asymmetry
  • Vascular occlusion signs (blanching, pain)
