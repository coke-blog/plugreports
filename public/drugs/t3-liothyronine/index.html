Liothyronine (T3)

Street names: T3, Cytomel, Thyroxyl

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • Active thyroid hormone — raises basal metabolic rate sharply
  • Weight drops fast; fat AND muscle burn
  • Legitimate use: hypothyroidism, myxedema

⚠Key risks

  • At abuse doses: cardiac arrhythmia, heart failure, bone loss
  • Muscle catabolism — the 'cut' comes with your muscle
  • Thyroid shutdown on cessation — rebound weight gain
  • Stacking with clenbuterol ('cutting stack') multiplies cardiac risk

✚Overdose signs

  • Racing irregular heartbeat
  • Tremor, sweating, fever
  • Chest pain, breathlessness — emergency
