Hydroxyzine (Vistaril / Atarax)

Street names: Vistaril, Atarax, Hydroxizine

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • Prescription antihistamine-anxiolytic: sedation, anxiety relief without addiction — the 'clean benzo alternative' doctors reach for

⚠Key risks

  • Not euphoric — dependence rare; the harm is oversedation and combos
  • QT-prolongation caution (heart rhythm)
  • Potentiates opioids, alcohol, benzos — the same combo deaths apply
  • Chronic use: dry mouth, weight gain, tolerance to sedation

✚Overdose signs

  • Severe sedation
  • Dry mouth, dilated pupils
  • Heart rhythm changes (rare)
