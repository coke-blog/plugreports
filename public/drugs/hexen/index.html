Hexen (N-Ethylhexedrone)

Street names: Hexen, NEH, Ethyl-hex

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • Euphoric stimulation, focus, sociability — 'budget cocaine'

⚠Key risks

  • Heavy binge compulsion
  • Vasoconstriction, cardiovascular strain
  • Sleep-deprivation psychosis
  • Nose damage from insufflation

✚Overdose signs

  • Racing heart, chest pain
  • Paranoia
  • Overheating
