Benzodiazepines

Alprazolam (Xanax 2 mg)

Street names: Xanax, Bars, Pfizer LongBars, Zans, Footballs

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • Fast anti-anxiety relief
  • Calm sedation
  • Muscle relaxation

⚠Key risks

  • Dependence within weeks; brutal withdrawal (seizures risk)
  • Counterfeit 'bars' increasingly contain fentanyl or bromazolam
  • Amnesia and risky behavior ('barred out')

✚Overdose signs

  • Extreme drowsiness
  • Slurred speech
  • Slow breathing — fatal with opioids/alcohol
