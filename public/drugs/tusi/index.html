Tusi ('Pink Cocaine')

Street names: Tusi, Tucibi, Pink cocaine, Pink powder, 2C-B of the poor (wrongly)

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • The 2023–2025 club scene's branded mystery: typically ketamine + MDMA + caffeine + a 2C-B-family compound + pink dye — a dissociative-empathogen mashup

⚠Key risks

  • THE LIE IS THE BRAND: 'Tusi' implies 2C-B but lab testing shows ever-changing mixtures — users cannot know the stimulant:dissociative ratio
  • Overdose pattern = stimulant toxidome + ketamine collapse in the same person
  • Colombia-Miami origin; spread through LatAm, Spain, US festivals
  • Because it's a 'brand,' users trust batches they shouldn't
  • Repeat-dose compounding of unknowns

✚Overdose signs

  • Overheating, racing heart (stimulant side)
  • Catatonic collapse (ketamine side)
  • Confusion between the two slows correct response
