Heroin

Street names: Dope, H, Smack, Sugar brown, Brown, Junk

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • Rapid intense euphoria ('rush')
  • Warmth, heavy limbs
  • Pain relief, drowsiness ('nodding')

⚠Key risks

  • Extremely addictive — physical dependence in weeks
  • Overdose: breathing slows or stops
  • Unknown strength + fentanyl contamination

✚Overdose signs

  • Unresponsive, pinpoint pupils
  • Breathing <8/min or gurgling
  • Blue/gray lips and fingernails

?Frequently asked questions

What does Heroin look like?

White/off-white powder (US) or brown base powder ('sugar brown'); black tar (Mexico)

What are the signs of a Heroin overdose?

Unresponsive, pinpoint pupils; Breathing <8/min or gurgling; Blue/gray lips and fingernails Call emergency services immediately and give naloxone if available — it reverses opioid overdoses.

How addictive is it?

Extremely addictive — physical dependence in weeks Dependence can develop with regular use.
