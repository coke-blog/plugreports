Anastrozole (Arimidex)

Street names: Arimidex, Adex

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • Aromatase inhibitor — lowers estrogen (used in breast cancer; abused in steroid cycles)

⚠Key risks

  • Bone density loss, joint pain with long-term use
  • Crash estrogen too low → depression, libido loss

✚Overdose signs

  • Nausea, headache
  • Hot flashes
