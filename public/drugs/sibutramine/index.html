Sibutramine (Meridia)

Street names: Meridia, Reductil

Overdose? Act now. Call emergency services — say "unresponsive, not breathing". Give naloxone for opioid-like signs. Hotlines

⚡What it does

  • SNRI appetite suppressant — real efficacy, which is why it's still illegally used

⚠Key risks

  • WITHDRAWN worldwide for cardiovascular events (strokes, heart attacks in the SCOUT trial)
  • Survives in adulterated supplements — buyers don't know they're taking it
  • Blood-pressure spikes, arrhythmia
  • Serotonin syndrome with antidepressants

✚Overdose signs

  • Racing heart, severe hypertension
  • Chest pain, stroke symptoms — emergency
  • Agitation, fever (serotonin syndrome)
