Kratom vs Suboxone

Updated 2026-09-21Sources: NIDA, FDA, SAMHSA

Kratom is a Southeast Asian leaf with opioid-like effects, sold legally in much of the US. Suboxone is the FDA-approved medication for opioid addiction. People constantly compare them — usually because they're self-treating withdrawal with one or the other.

The verdict

Kratom is a folk remedy with a real but modest effect and zero quality control; Suboxone is medicine with a track record. People use kratom to escape opioid dependence and frequently end up with a new, unregulated dependence — clinics now report treating kratom withdrawal. If you're choosing between them for opioid addiction, Suboxone is the evidence-backed choice; if you're using kratom casually, know that concentrates and 7-OH products behave like real opioids.

?Frequently asked questions

Can kratom help with Suboxone withdrawal?

Some people report it helps, but buprenorphine withdrawal is long, and kratom adds its own dependence risk. A supervised taper is safer than layering substances.

Is kratom safer than Suboxone?

Suboxone is safer per dose (ceiling effect, known contents). Kratom's plant variability and contaminant history make it the wild card — 'natural' doesn't mean predictable.
