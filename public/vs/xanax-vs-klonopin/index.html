Xanax vs Klonopin

Xanax (alprazolam) and Klonopin (clonazepam) are both benzos, but they behave differently: Xanax is a fast spark that burns out quickly, Klonopin is a slow burn that lasts all day. Klonopin is also used for seizures and nerve pain.

Xanax vs Klonopin — side by side

XanaxKlonopin
What it isFast, short benzo for panic and anxietyLong-acting benzo for anxiety, seizures and nerve pain

Overdose dangerDeadly with opioids, alcohol, sleep medsIdentical — it just takes longer to wear off

Price & legalRx-only; ~$1–5 per pill divertedRx-only; ~$1–4 per pill diverted

The verdict

Klonopin's long duration makes it somewhat less compulsive day-to-day, which is why it's often picked for long-term treatment. But it builds up in the body, and its withdrawal can start late and last weeks. Xanax is more binge-prone. Both are deadly in combination with opioids or alcohol and both need a tapered, supervised exit.

?Frequently asked questions

Which lasts longer, Xanax or Klonopin?

Klonopin, by far — 8–12 hours versus Xanax's 4–6. That's why Klonopin is dosed once or twice a day.

Is Klonopin stronger than Xanax?

Per milligram, similar. 0.25 mg of Xanax ≈ 0.25–0.5 mg of Klonopin. The difference is speed and length, not strength.
