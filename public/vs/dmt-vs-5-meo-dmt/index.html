DMT vs 5-MeO-DMT

Updated 2026-09-21Sources: NIDA, MAPS

5-MeO-DMT (Bufo / Toad Venom)
Psychedelics

DMT and 5-MeO-DMT are sister molecules found in different plants and animals (5-MeO famously in the Sonoran Desert toad). DMT is visual — geometry, colors, entities. 5-MeO is formless — a white-out of pure consciousness where there's nothing left to see.

The verdict

DMT shows you something; 5-MeO removes you entirely. Users call 5-MeO 'the white light' — no entities, no geometry, no self. Both last minutes and both are physically safe in isolation, but 5-MeO's combination of instant total incapacitation and nausea makes a sober sitter mandatory; several documented deaths involved people left alone. Of the two, DMT is far more studied and far more forgiving.

?Frequently asked questions

Is 5-MeO-DMT stronger than DMT?

In terms of ego dissolution, yes — most users rank it beyond DMT. In terms of content, it has less: where DMT delivers a cosmos, 5-MeO delivers a white-out.

Is smoking toad venom dangerous?

The molecule itself has no lethal overdose, but the experience causes immediate unconsciousness and sometimes vomiting. Every safety guideline says: never alone, never lying on the back, experienced sitter present.
