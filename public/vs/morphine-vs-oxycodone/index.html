Morphine vs Oxycodone

Updated 2026-09-21Sources: NIDA, WHO, FDA

Morphine is the original opioid — used since the Civil War and still the reference standard doctors measure every other painkiller against. Oxycodone is its smoother, slightly stronger synthetic descendant. Morphine is the ruler; oxycodone is what usually gets diverted.

The verdict

Oxycodone is essentially morphine with better marketing and a cleaner feel — which is precisely why it overtook morphine as the misused pill of choice. Medically, morphine remains the gold standard for severe and palliative pain, and every strength chart (MME) is built on it. For misuse, neither has any safety edge: same receptor, same breathing-stopping overdose, same withdrawal.

?Frequently asked questions

Is morphine stronger than oxycodone?

No — oxycodone is about 1.5 times stronger per milligram. Morphine is simply the yardstick everything else is measured against.

Why do hospitals still use morphine?

It's predictable, cheap, and deeply understood after 150+ years of use — especially in palliative care and heart-attack pain.
