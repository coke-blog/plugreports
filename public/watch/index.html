Watch — what drugs actually look like

Short, close-up identification clips. What a photo can’t show — texture, clumping, shine — a few seconds of video can. But remember: looks prove nothing about purity or safety. Fentanyl is invisible in any of this. Always test.

Why watch instead of just read?Most overdose deaths involve a substance the person could not identify. These clips give “what does X look like” a visual answer — but never use appearance to judge safety. See how to spot pressed pills and keep a hotline handy.
