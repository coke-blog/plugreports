# Backlink Outreach — Tier 1 Template & Target List

*Goal: get plugreports pages listed on harm-reduction org resource pages, campus wellness pages, and directories. These pages exist to link resources like ours — this is the highest-acceptance outreach there is. 5–10 emails/week, personalized first line per org.*

---

## The email template

**Subject:** Free harm-reduction resource for your [resource page / directory]

> Hi [Name],
>
> I maintain plugreports.com, a free harm-reduction library — [463 plain-English drug profiles](https://plugreports.com/drugs/), verified hotline directories for [9 regions](https://plugreports.com/hotlines/), and data trackers sourced from DEA NFLIS and the CDC.
>
> I think two of our pages would genuinely fit your [resource page / links section]:
>
> - [Fentanyl Adulterant Tracker](https://plugreports.com/data/fentanyl-adulterants/) — what is actually being mixed into the fentanyl supply right now (xylazine, medetomidine, BTMPS), with dated NFLIS/CDC sources. Built for citation by journalists and educators.
> - [How to spot pressed pills](https://plugreports.com/topics/spot-pressed-pills/) — the visual guide your audience keeps forwarding.
>
> Everything is free, no account required, no ads. If you list it, great; if not, no follow-up from me.
>
> [Name]
> plugreports.com

**Rules that keep this out of the spam folder:**
- Personalize the first line (mention their actual program/city — "saw your Philly wound-care program mentioned…").
- ONE link per email max when cold; the two-link version is for warmer follow-ups.
- Never send the same text twice — vary the subject and opening.
- No follow-up more than once, 7+ days later, two sentences max.
